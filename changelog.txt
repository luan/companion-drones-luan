Version: 4.0.0
Date: 2026-10-10
  Features:
    - Rename the mod to Entourage, preserving its mod ID and save data.
    - Add distinct Blender-rendered companion, utility and plasma interceptor models, icons and cover artwork.
    - Animate smooth turning, duct rotors, plasma exhaust and deploying utility landing gear.
    - Apply companion paint to armor masks across the crew, preserving unpainted hardware.
    - Give each player a permanent companion that follows between planets and survives death.
    - Deploy up to six managers with exact task batches and private construction inventories.
    - Deploy six independent stationary crafters, with batch planning and overhead progress.
    - Launch custom combat escorts while the companion continues construction work.
    - Use supplies from the player, vehicle, companion and all chests in the owner's network.
    - Add expensive development research for every non-Nauvis planet, including modded planets.
    - Grant another companion per two developed planets, plus infinite promethium expansion.
    - Start crafting at configurable 2x speed; research improves speed and crafter capacity.
  Changes:
    - Default mini drone movement to half speed with an immediately adjustable runtime setting.
    - Remove fuel, charging delays and obsolete drone/equipment crafting recipes.
    - Preserve legacy items, equipment and mode settings for existing saves.
    - Separate gentle cyan construction beams from red combat lasers.
    - Keep companions near the owner and suspend their work on space platforms.
    - Skip blocked or unaffordable work without monopolizing the task queue.
  Bugfixes:
    - Remove competing native flight steering to keep following, stopping and turning smooth.
    - Reserve exact item counts instead of taking all matching inventory items.
    - Preserve full-chest salvage, repair-pack durability and cargo awaiting return space.
    - Cache supply and recipe checks during dispatch to avoid repeated inventory scans.
    - Retire old-force combat escorts and recall crew assignments when the owner changes force.
    - Honor the construction toggle for active crafters and handle invalid migration entities.
    - Prevent old drone blueprints from bypassing researched fleet capacity.

      the required table-parameter form instead of the old positional arguments.
